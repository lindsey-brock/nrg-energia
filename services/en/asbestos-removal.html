What we offer

Complete asbestos remediation services

Registered with the National Environmental Managers Register, we offer a complete asbestos removal and remediation service for roofing, replacing it with new lightweight structures made from durable, practical materials with high energy performance.

Asbestos risk assessment

Inspection of asbestos-containing materials (ACM), full site survey and remediation plan in compliance with current regulations.

Active waste cycle management

Authorised Cat. 5 transport and disposal at licensed facilities.

Roofing renewal with premium materials

Replacement of asbestos roofing with new lightweight, durable and energy-efficient structures.

How we work

Our process

Site survey & consultation
Our team supports the client from the very first site survey, offering comprehensive technical advice based on our extensive experience in the sector.
Regulatory compliance
We follow every phase in full compliance with current regulations, with particular attention to health and safety and environmental requirements.
Documentation & paperwork
We handle the preparation of all required documents and communications to the relevant authorities, including the mandatory ASL notification within the statutory timeframe.
Complete organisational process
We coordinate the entire organisational process — from project planning through to the handover of final documentation — ensuring transparency and compliance at every stage.
